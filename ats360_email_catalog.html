ATS360 Email Template Catalog

Review the default system email templates triggered throughout the recruitment lifecycle. This catalog outlines when each message is sent and its primary objective to help you visualize candidate communication. Click on any image to expand it.

Candidate Communication
Job Invite Email
Trigger

Manually initiated by a Recruiter clicking "Send Candidates Email Invite" for applicants on the Shortlisted list.

Objective

To proactively reach out to past or sourced candidates whose profiles match a new opening, inviting them to learn more and apply.

Candidate Communication
Job Application Confirmation
Trigger

Automatically sent when an applicant clicks the "Apply" button and submits their information via the Career Portal.

Objective

To confirm receipt of the candidate's application, thank them for their interest, and set expectations regarding next steps and potential interviews.

Internal HR Alert
New Applicant Notification
Trigger

Automatically sent when a new application is successfully received through the Career Portal.

Objective

To alert the internal hiring team or recruiter that a new candidate has applied, providing a quick summary of their profile and a direct link to view their record.

Candidate Communication
Rejection Email
Trigger

Sent when a candidate's application stage is officially moved to "Rejected."

Objective

To professionally and courteously inform the candidate that the team has chosen not to move forward with their application at this time.

Candidate Communication
Interview Request
Trigger

Sent when a candidate is selected to advance to the interview stage.

Objective

To congratulate the candidate on advancing and prompt them to select their preferred interview date and time from available options.

Candidate Communication
Interview Invite (Confirmation)
Trigger

Sent once an interview date, time, and timezone are mutually agreed upon and confirmed.

Objective

To provide the candidate with an official calendar event containing meeting links, participant details, and instructions for their upcoming interview.

Candidate Communication
Opening Expired – Auto Rejection
Trigger

Automatically triggered if a job opening reaches its designated expiration date without action, automatically closing the opening.

Objective

To notify any remaining active applicants that the job opening has expired and that they are no longer being considered for that specific requisition.

Candidate Communication
Availability for Interview
Trigger

Sent when HR/Recruiters need to coordinate scheduling constraints before formalizing the interview.

Objective

To invite the candidate to share their availability through a provided link so the team can align schedules.

Internal HR Alert
Bulk Application Share
Trigger

Sent when a recruiter manually selects multiple candidates and chooses to share them internally.

Objective

To efficiently send a batch of pre-screened candidate profiles to a Hiring Manager for review, complete with applicant details and links to their full records.

Candidate Communication
Offer Email Body
Trigger

Manually composed and sent when an applicant is moved to the "Offer" stage and an employment contract is generated.

Objective

To formally extend an offer of employment, expressing excitement to welcome them to the team, and attaching their contract for review and signature.

Candidate Communication
Follow-up Email
Trigger

Manually triggered by the recruiter from the "Received" or "In-Progress" stage.

Objective

To touch base with the candidate regarding their application status, inform them of upcoming evaluations, and keep communication channels open.
